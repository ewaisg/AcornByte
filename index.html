A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
begin.

I know the problem, but not the solution. Is that enough?

Absolutely. Describe what you want to accomplish and what is happening now. You don’t need to choose the technology before we talk.

Can you work with tools I already use?

That’s a good place to start. We can review your current spreadsheets, software, and process before deciding whether an improvement or something new makes sense.

How does pricing work?

Pricing depends on the scope. After we understand the need, we’ll agree on the work and price before starting. Software, hardware, or other outside costs will be discussed separately.

Do you offer remote and in-person help?

Consultations are available by phone or Teams. In-person help in the Erie / Denver area is by arrangement, depending on the location and project. AcornByte is a home-based and online business; there is no walk-in office.

How should I share an example or a problem?

Start with a short description. Please don’t email passwords, financial details, identity documents, or confidential client files. If a sample would help, we’ll discuss how to share an appropriate version.
