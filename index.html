FREQUENTLY ASKED

Getting
started

What products does AcornByte offer?

We offer Stampit (AI invoice approval platform, in early access) and FaciliOne (AI project command center, in development). We also build custom AI and ML solutions for businesses with specialized requirements.

Who is AcornByte built for?

Our solutions work for businesses of any size across any industry. From startups to enterprise, if you need to automate workflows, extract insights from documents, or streamline operations with AI, we can help.

What technology does AcornByte use?

Our platform is built on AWS with industry-leading AI services including Amazon Textract for document intelligence, Bedrock for AI capabilities, and S3 for secure cloud storage. All solutions feature enterprise-grade security.

How do I access Stampit?

Stampit is currently in development with early access available. Email ewaisg@acornbyte.com with "Stampit Early Access Request" to learn more about accessing the platform.

Does AcornByte build custom solutions?

Yes. Beyond our platform products, we develop custom AI and ML applications tailored to your specific workflows. Contact us to discuss your requirements.

What industries does AcornByte serve?

AcornByte serves businesses across all industries. Our AI and ML solutions are designed to be industry-agnostic, applicable to any business looking to automate processes and extract insights.
